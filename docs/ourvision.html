OUR VISION

The Newhaven Heritage and Cultural Centre will not only be a place of learning, a place of engagement, and a place of entertainment — it will also be a place of resource.

Could Newhaven once again have its own museum? With Victoria School soon to become vacant, there is an exciting opportunity for the site to become a community asset. Newhaven Heritage believes the Anchor Annexe would make an ideal Heritage and Cultural Centre, offering modern facilities, full accessibility, climate‑controlled spaces, and outdoor areas that could become welcoming community gardens.

Edinburgh is now a year‑round tourist destination, and Newhaven Heritage knows how popular the former community museum was, attracting over 30,000 visitors annually. With growing visitor numbers — including cruise passengers — a new heritage centre could once again make Newhaven a key destination. A small shop would support local artisans by selling locally made products and souvenirs.

Newhaven Heritage aims to create a world‑class community museum and cultural centre that combines innovation, engaging displays, and strong community involvement. The centre would celebrate Newhaven’s unique history while helping visitors understand how this independent community thrived alongside a major city. Any surplus income would be reinvested into projects supporting residents and strengthening community identity, continuing the tradition of local care once embodied by the Ancient Free Fishermen’s Society of Newhaven.

Whether or not our vision comes to fruition depends on public opinion. A counter‑bid proposes using the Anchor Building for pre‑school years run by a private nursery; Newhaven Heritage seeks to use it as a place of learning, engagement, entertainment, and resource. If our vision appeals to you, please contact the Councillors who will make the final decision. With construction delays pushing the new school’s opening to Summer 2021, it is not too late to influence the outcome.
