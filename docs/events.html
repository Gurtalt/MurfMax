PHOENIX PROJECT

The Phoenix Project, created with support from Newhaven Heritage, helps local pupils explore Newhaven’s past by researching real children who lived and went to school there over a hundred years ago. It blends history with creativity, letting students act as young detectives while learning about the community’s traditions and values. The name reflects the school’s move to a new building — like a phoenix rising — symbolizing renewal while keeping Newhaven’s spirit alive.
