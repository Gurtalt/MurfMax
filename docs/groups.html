THE COMMUNITY
GARDENS GROUP

The Community Gardens Group supports Newhaven Heritage on Fort by bringing residents together to grow, restore, and celebrate local green spaces. We share updates, volunteer opportunities, and gardening knowledge to strengthen community connections and protect Newhaven’s unique heritage.

THE NEWHAVEN
COMMUNITY CHOIR

The Community Choir brings Newhaven residents together to celebrate local heritage through shared singing. We welcome all voices, encourage creativity, and build community spirit with rehearsals, performances, and events that honour the cultural identity and history of the Fort.

THE NEWHAVEN
COMMUNITY
HISTORY GROUP

The Newhaven Community History Group preserves and shares the rich stories of our village and Fort. We explore local heritage, collect memories, and encourage residents to contribute photos, research, and family histories that strengthen community identity and celebrate Newhaven’s past.
